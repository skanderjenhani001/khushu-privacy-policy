Privacy Policy for Khushu

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application "Khushu - Minimalist Dhikr & Tasbih" (the "Application"), developed and maintained by Skander Jenhani ("Developer").

1. Information Collection and Storage

Khushu is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture:

  • No Personal Data Collected: The Application does not collect, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical location, or device identifiers).
  • Local On-Device Storage: All user preferences, counters, daily streaks, and custom settings are stored locally on your device using encrypted, offline storage. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any external servers.

2. Third-Party Services & Permissions

  • Khushu does not use third-party advertising SDKs, tracking tools, or external analytics frameworks.
  • Device permissions requested (such as vibration/haptics) are strictly used to deliver core application features and functionality.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all app data resides solely in local storage on your device, deleting the application or clearing its application storage via Android system settings permanently removes all stored data.

4. Children’s Privacy

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13, nor does it knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.
